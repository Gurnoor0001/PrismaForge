## ⚡ The Vision
PrismaForge is not just a filter—it is a **Neural Reality Engine**. By utilizing an advanced VGG19 Encoder-Decoder architecture, the app mathematically extracts the "artistic DNA" of any style reference (like a Van Gogh painting or a pencil sketch) and weaves it directly into the structural geometry of your uploaded photos. 

The result? Breathtaking, high-definition AI art generated entirely offline.

## 🧠 Core Architecture
At the heart of PrismaForge lies **AdaIN (Adaptive Instance Normalization)**. Traditional style transfer algorithms require hours of retraining for every single new painting. PrismaForge eliminates this limitation completely:
* **VGG19 Feature Extraction:** Slices images into multi-dimensional feature maps.
* **AdaIN Alignment:** Instantly aligns the mean and variance of your content image with the artistic style.
* **Decoder Synthesis:** Reconstructs the image from the aligned feature maps back into reality.

## ✨ Highlighted Features
- **🔮 Arbitrary Style Synthesis:** No restrictions. Upload *any* photo and *any* art style.
- **🎛️ Dynamic Resolution Scaling:** Instantly swap between **Standard (512px)** for thick, highly abstract brush strokes, or **Ultra (1024px)** for hyper-detailed, high-definition stylization.
- **🎚️ Intensity Control:** Dial in the exact stylization strength (0-100%) to perfectly balance reality with art.
- **💎 Glassmorphic UI:** A breathtaking, state-of-the-art interface featuring neon-cyberpunk dark mode, `VanillaTilt.js` 3D floating cards, and smooth micro-animations.

## 🖼️ Transformation Pipeline
The workflow is designed to be as seamless as possible:
1. **Source Image:** Upload a clear, well-lit photo.
2. **Style Reference:** Upload your favorite artistic medium (oil paint, sketch, watercolor, abstract).
3. **Neural Forge:** The PyTorch backend processes and aligns the tensors in real-time.
4. **Masterpiece:** Download your mathematically perfect artistic fusion directly from the browser.
